About Clearia

One job: find what payers underpaid, and get it back.

Clearia is a specialist recovery firm for independent medical practices. We don't do billing, we don't sell software, and we don't take engagements the data doesn't support. We audit what payers paid against what contracts specify — and pursue the difference through the payers' own processes.

Request the free diagnostic or write to contact@cleariagroup.com

Why we exist

Commercial payers underpay practices by 1 to 3 percent of net revenue on average — and that average is held down by hospital systems with entire teams watching for it. In independent practices, where nobody watches, the number runs higher: one $20M practice that checked found 6 percent.

That money survives because every underpaid claim closes at a zero balance, looking paid. Billing teams keep claims moving; nobody is staffed to re-check finished business against contracted rates. We built Clearia to be the party that looks.

How we work

  • White glove, in person. We work at your practice, not from a call center — and we study payer behavior for a living.
  • On the payer's side of the ledger only. We never touch claim submission, posting, or your billing workflow — and nothing we do audits your team's work.
  • Your name, your control. You see every finding and decide what we pursue; appeals go out under the practice's name.
  • By the book. Underpayment reconsiderations are a routine, contractual process. We use the dispute mechanisms your contracts already provide — professionally, with the paper trail to match.

The incentive structure

The diagnostic is free, and our fee is a percentage of dollars actually recovered — nothing upfront, ever. We carry the risk of being wrong, which is exactly why we won't take an engagement unless the diagnostic shows real recoverable dollars.

Our incentives and yours point the same direction: worst case, you get a free expert read of your payment data.
