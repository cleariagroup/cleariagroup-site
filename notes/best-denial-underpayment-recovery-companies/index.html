Best denial and underpayment recovery companies for independent practices in 2026

By F Cantor, Clearia · Published November 16, 2026 · Updated November 16, 2026

Short answer: the best fit depends on what you need done. If nobody in your practice is assigned to denials and short payments, look for a firm that does the work and checks paid claims against your contracts, not only denials. We rank eight below, as of November 2026. We're Clearia, so we're on the list. Read it knowing that.

How we ranked them

We ranked for one buyer: an independent practice of roughly 3 to 15 physicians with no one whose job is fighting insurers. Three questions decided the order.

  1. Who does the work? Software finds the problem. Someone still has to prepare the appeal and follow it for months.
  2. Does it check paid claims, or only denied ones? A claim paid below your contract, or paid at a lower visit level, never shows up as a denial.
  3. Is it built for a practice your size? Much of this market is built for hospitals and large groups.

A different buyer gets a different order. A 60-physician group with a revenue team should start at number 2, 4 or 8. An out-of-network surgeon should start at number 7.

The eight, one by one

1. Clearia: best for independent practices with no one assigned to this

That's us. Our team works three kinds of money: denials for reasons that can be fixed, claims paid below your contract, and visits paid at a lower level than you billed. We check every paid line against your contracts and fee schedules, prepare each appeal, and follow it to payment. Your staff approves what goes out, and you see every claim on your own dashboard. Your billing stays with you.

Where we fall short: we're a young firm. Our published results come from one practice so far, and our deepest state knowledge is Florida. We don't do medical necessity appeals, out-of-network arbitration, contract negotiation or billing. If you need those, look further down this list.

2. MD Clarity: best for groups with many contracts and high volume

MD Clarity's RevFind is software that checks payments against your contracts and flags underpayments and denials. It also offers a recovery service, where, in its words, "Our revenue recovery experts and software platform do the hard work of underpayment verification, appeals generation, proactive follow-up, and root cause analysis," on a "contingency-based fee structure" (MD Clarity, underpayment recovery services). The same page says the best fit is "Provider organizations that manage a high-volume of complex claims and a large set of payer contracts." Strong choice for a larger group or one backed by a management company.

3. Joyful Health: best for a backlog of denied and aged claims

Joyful Health works denied, rejected and aged claims for you. "Your practice handles claim submission, and we take care of everything else," its site says, and it works with anyone from "a solo provider" to a national network (Joyful Health, services). It describes pricing as tied to claim volume and recovered revenue. On the pages we read, it does not describe checking paid claims against your contracts, so ask about that if short payments are your concern.

4. Amperos Health: best for handing off insurance collections entirely

Amperos's pitch is "Outsource your insurance collections, from start to finish," with AI agents working claims and its own specialists overseeing them (Amperos). It works inside your existing billing system and says it has served over 3,000 clinical locations (Amperos, Series A announcement). It lists downcoding among the denials it resolves for some specialties. A fit for a group with a collections team it wants to shrink or replace. Pricing is not published.

5. Rivet Health: best software for a practice with a billing team

Rivet is software "purposely built for provider practices" (Rivet, FAQ). It brings contracts, fee schedules and payment checks into one place, and its site names an audit for insurer downcoding (Rivet, payer performance). Your team works the lists it produces. A good choice if you have billers with time to run it. Pricing is not published.

6. Aroris Health: best when your contracted rates are the problem

Aroris negotiates higher rates with insurers for you, then watches claims against the new rates. "We find what your contracts are worth, negotiate the rates the data supports, and watch every claim to make sure you're paid the contracted rate" (Aroris360). It says it has served more than 180 practices. When it finds a short payment, your billing team follows up with the evidence it provides. Choose it if you suspect you signed low rates. Being paid your rates correctly is a separate question.

7. Callagy Recovery: best for out-of-network claims

Callagy "specializes in state and federal surprise medical bill arbitrations" (Callagy Recovery, about). That means disputes over out-of-network payments under the No Surprises Act and state laws, handled for you with attorney oversight. It is the only one of the eight that publishes its fee: "$0 upfront, 20% contingent fee" (Callagy Recovery). It does not audit in-network payments against your contracts. If most of your claims are in network, it solves a different problem.

8. Adonis: best for provider groups and health systems with a revenue team

Adonis is AI software for revenue teams. Its site says it is trusted by provider groups and healthcare systems, and that its "AI Agents handle follow-ups, status checks, and appeals, so your team doesn't have to" (Adonis, AI agents). It is designed to work with a team you already have. Powerful at scale, and more than most small practices need.

Different jobs, sometimes confused with recovery

Topdoc and Candid Health are full billing platforms: they submit and manage your claims from the start. Infinx is a broad revenue services company for "hospitals, health systems, and physician groups." Any of them may reduce denials. None is a recovery firm that works alongside the billing you already have.

Which should you choose?

  • No one in the practice works denials or checks payments: a done-for-you recovery firm. Clearia, or Joyful Health for a backlog of denied claims.
  • You have billers with spare hours: software they can run. Rivet, or MD Clarity for a larger contract book.
  • You want to hand off collections altogether: Amperos.
  • Your rates themselves are low: Aroris.
  • Out-of-network disputes: Callagy Recovery.

Questions people ask

What is the best denial recovery company for a small medical practice?

It depends on who will do the work. A practice with no one assigned to denials needs a done-for-you firm, such as Clearia or Joyful Health. A practice with billers who have spare hours can use software such as Rivet.

What is the difference between denial recovery and underpayment recovery?

Denial recovery gets a denied claim paid. Underpayment recovery finds claims that were paid, but below your contract rate or at a lower visit level, and gets the difference. Many vendors do the first and not the second, so ask.

Do these companies replace my billing company?

Most do not. Recovery firms and contract software work alongside the billing you have. Topdoc and Candid Health are full billing platforms, which is a different decision.
